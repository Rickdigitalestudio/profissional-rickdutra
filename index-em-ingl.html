Frequently asked questions

Before you talk to me

How much does a website cost?

Landing page from R$ 697, complete business website from R$ 997 and online store from R$ 2,497. Logo from R$ 200, LinkedIn from R$ 297 and ABNT formatting from R$ 120. The final price comes from a written proposal with scope and deadline fixed before any payment.

What is the delivery time?

Landing page in 5 business days, business website in 10 business days and online store in 15 business days (counted from payment confirmation and receipt of your materials: texts, photos and logo). Need it sooner? Urgent deadlines are possible — just tell me and I will check.

Are the testimonials real?

Yes. Every testimonial on this site is published on the client website I built, and the link on the card takes you there to check. They are quotes from end users of those sites (gym members, hotel guests, barbershop clients). When the business owners send their own testimonial about the work, it goes here too.

How long until my site shows on Google?

Indexing is requested on launch day. Rankings start appearing in 2 to 8 weeks, depending on competition in your niche and investment in content.

What is included in the price?

Responsive layout (phone, tablet and desktop), on-page SEO optimized code, publishing with HTTPS, Google Search Console setup and 2 rounds of revision per stage. Domain, paid hosting and third-party image licenses are on you, unless agreed otherwise in the proposal.

How does payment work?

50% upfront to start the project and 50% on final delivery, via PIX or bank transfer. You receive a written proposal with scope, deadline and price before the first payment. If I do not deliver by the agreed deadline, the agreement stands — it is in the Terms of Service.

Do you do maintenance after the site is ready?

Yes. The first 30 days of tweaks are included in the delivery. After that there is an optional maintenance plan from R$ 297/month with updates, backup and WhatsApp support — or you go on your own, because the code is yours and documented.

Will the website really be mine?

Yes. After full payment you receive the source code, the files and the usage rights. My portfolio keeps showing the work — that is the only point where I ask for permission.

Do you work with my city?

Yes, I work with all of Brazil 100% online: briefing by WhatsApp, approval on screen and delivery with the site live. I am based in São Paulo (SP) and work with clients from several states.
